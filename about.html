Since 2019

A discipline of three tones.

JOUD began as a rejection of noise — of endless seasonal color stories and disposable cloth. We chose black, white, and gold, and have spent every collection since proving that restraint is not a limitation, but a craft.

Our Approach

Fewer pieces, worn longer.

Each garment begins with cloth, not sketches. We source heavyweight cotton, brushed fleece, and structured wool-blends first, then design around what the material wants to do.

Every release is small and considered. We would rather sell out of a piece we're proud of than restock something built to be replaced.

What We Stand For

Values

01

Material First

We choose cloth before silhouette. If the fabric isn't right, the design doesn't move forward.

02

Restraint as Craft

Black, white, and gold — a fixed palette that forces every decision to be about cut, weight, and finish.

03

Built to Outlast

Reinforced seams, considered hardware, and finishes designed to age well rather than wear out.
